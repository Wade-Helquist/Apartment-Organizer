# Grinny User Guide

Grinny is a personal learning app for the topic you're studying. You describe what you're interested in, and it lays the topic out so you can follow it as far as you like. Everything you learn is kept as small facts you can browse, review and ask questions about.

Version shown at the top right (for example `v3.80`). It matches the number of the latest publish.

---

## The footer: always on screen

The footer is fixed to the bottom of every page and has two parts.

**The GRINNY buttons**

| Button | Tab | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **G** | Gather | Look up a topic and explore it |
| **R** | Revisit | Pick up things you looked at before |
| **I** | Inquire | Ask questions answered from your own facts |
| **N** | Nexus | Browse and search all your facts |
| **N** | Notes | Open one fact for more detail and a memory trick |
| **Y** | You | Your interests, learning topics and AI cost |
| 📌 | Learning / Facts | Switch between only the facts you marked and all facts |

**The ask bar** sits under the buttons on every page. Its hint changes to "Ask about …" plus the last topic you were reading. See [Ask bar](#ask-bar).

## Gather

1. Type what you're interested in, in your own words, in the single box. A few words or a sentence both work. Example: `how stress affects bipolar disorder`.
2. Press **Go**. If you wrote more than three words, or a question, Grinny first turns it into a short topic phrase that keeps every detail you mentioned. The title letters rearrange while it works.

You then get a page for the term. From top to bottom:

- **What you already know.** A short summary built only from facts you've already saved about it.
- **The term line.** A short description. Tap it to keep the facts it contains. A ✓ means they're saved.
- **Chip groups.** Categories, Topics, Related words and Meanings. Tap a chip to keep what it says as facts, or tap the term inside it to look that up.
- **Explore.** The main way to learn. See below.
- **Find a kind of fact.** A dropdown of 13 fact types. See below.
- **New connections.** Links Grinny finds between this term and facts you already have.

### Explore: branches that radiate from one idea

Explore starts with a short story paragraph about your term. Under it, **4 to 6 coloured branch cards** spread out in different directions, each with a one-line hint.

- Tap a card to open it. Its own paragraph and its own branches appear inside it. The other cards stay on screen, so you can see the whole spread.
- Tap an open card again to close it.
- Keep going as deep as you want. The step crumbs show the path you took.
- When a topic is specific enough, it gives facts instead of more branches.
- Every step stays on your original term, never another meaning of the words.

### Find a kind of fact (13 types)

Under each term is a dropdown. Pick a type and Grinny finds 5 to 6 facts of that kind about the term, shows them, and keeps them.

Numbers & Stats · Time Travel · How Things Work · Mind-Benders · Freaks of Nature · Life Hacks · Fun Trivia · Brain Upgrades · Myth-Busters · Hidden Links · Origin Stories · Human Systems · General Reference

## Facts, 📌 and the Learning / Facts button

Everything you read produces **atomic facts**: one small claim each, such as *Cow rumen — holds about — 150 liters*. They are kept automatically.

- **📌 (marked)** means "a thing I'm learning". Tap the circle on a fact to mark it. Tap again to unmark.
- **Green 📌** is a suggestion. Grinny marks new facts in green when they relate to facts you already marked. Tap one to remove it, tap again to accept it. **Approve all** accepts every green one at once. Green marks clear when you leave the app, and they don't spread to more facts.
- **Learning / Facts button.**
  - **Learning** shows only marked facts in Revisit, Nexus, Inquire and Notes.
  - **Facts** shows everything.

Topics from your marked facts also steer the AI, which leans toward building on them.

---

## Revisit

A list of your searches, new chains and clicked ideas, newest first. Tap one to search it again in Gather. Any entry that has a marked fact in it is kept across sessions.

## Inquire

Ask a question and get an answer built **only from your own facts**. It follows the Learning / Facts button, so with Learning on it answers from marked facts only.

## Nexus

Browse everything you know.

- **Search** to filter.
- **Sort** by original term, original term A to Z, keywords A to Z, keywords by number of facts, longest chain or newest.
- **Stated / Inferred** filter. Stated facts were said in an answer. Inferred ones were worked out by the AI.
- Tap a keyword to open its facts.

## Notes

1. Search or scroll the list of facts.
2. Tap one. Grinny shows:
   - **More**: two to four sentences of extra information.
   - **How to remember it**: a mnemonic or vivid image.
   - **Real pages**: a link that searches Google for the exact phrase.
3. Opening a note also saves the new facts its "More" text states.
4. **Mark learning** marks the fact with 📌.

## You

- **My interests.** Write what you're curious about, then tap **Save**. Every AI answer leans toward it. **Seed now** asks you a question based on your interests.
- **Learning now.** The topics from your marked facts. Tap × to stop learning one.
- **AI cost.** What this session has cost and each person's overall cost.

---

## Ask bar

Type a question in the bar at the bottom. A large chat panel rises over the page.

- It knows what you were just reading.
- Answers alternate between two kinds of story paragraph. One **opens up broadly**, with categories, ideas and things to consider. The next goes **specific**, with the topic's most unusual facts and narrower topics. The topics in quotes are tappable and ask a follow-up.
- Every answer saves its atomic facts. A small line shows "+N facts saved".
- Tap × to close the panel. Moving to another tab also closes it.

## What the AI is told

- Grinny is for oddities and the unusual: surprising, thought-provoking things.
- No common-sense choices or advice.
- Every fact is one claim, with a value of eight words or fewer.
- Your interests lean the examples toward what you care about.

## AI keys and cost

- Every AI call goes through **OpenAI** using the key stored in the app's database. If that fails, it falls back to **Claude**. Claude calls aren't priced.
- Cost is tracked per person. You can see your own session and overall cost in **You**.
- The **owner** can tap the version tag at the top right to see a cost-by-person table, with a **Back** button to return.

## Sharing

The app is private to you and the people you invite. Invited people can use it with the same stored key. Anyone you invite can read that key from the app's database, so only invite people you trust.

## Tips

- Be specific. `how octopus arms think on their own` gives a sharper page than `octopus`.
- Open several branches in Explore before leaving. Every opened step keeps its facts.
- Mark the few facts you really want to learn, and set the toggle to **Learning** to study just those.
- Use Inquire before an exam or a conversation to pull everything you know about a question together.

## Things to know

- Grinny works on facts it has written itself with AI. Check anything important.
- The title is Grinny with a small superscript number on the right. It hides while the title rearranges.
- If a step says "Couldn't load", tap it again to retry.
